## Prompt keranjang/tas

> Edit target: attached Sparkleen design board. Extract ONLY the 3D blue laundry basket with folded white towels and the white shopping tote with blue handles shown inside the LEFT phone's blue hero card. Deliver the isolated 3D objects as a single production-ready cutout, generously filling the canvas, on a genuinely transparent background. Preserve the reference objects' cobalt blue and white palette, soft studio light, polished rounded 3D style and relative arrangement: basket left, tote right, folded towels behind. The tote has a tiny blue white washer symbol. Remove ALL interface, phones, text, buttons, backdrop panels and other objects. Do not render any UI or text. Crisp clean alpha edges, subtle contact shading only under the objects, no square shadow tile. This asset will be displayed at 160 pixels wide in the app.
